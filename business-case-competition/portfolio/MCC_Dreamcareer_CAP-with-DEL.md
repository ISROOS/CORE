## Storyline / argument pattern
- Classic consulting arc: **Background → Problem identification (problem tree) → Market/competitor → Solution reveal → Solution deep-dives → Financials & risk → Conclusion**.
- **Problem-to-solution mapping on one slide** (p4): KQ → 3 problems → 2 root causes each → 3 sub-initiatives per solution letter. This is the structural backbone and the strongest page.
- **Branded acronym:** **CAP** (Climate–Advanced–Proactive) "with DEL", with the tagline "CAP Strategy with POWER to strengthen operational resilience". The three letters map one-to-one to the three problems (climate output loss, untapped premium revenue, carbon cost volatility).
- Build/Buy/Hybrid framing gives each pillar a strategic-mode label.
- There is no persona or emotional hook, and no explicit "ask" or decision request at the end.

## Frameworks & analyses used
Problem tree (KQ → problem → root cause → solution); problem prioritisation strip; industry overview with policy drivers (PESTEL-lite: NZE 2060, Nilai Ekonomi Karbon); business model / revenue stream description; market table with CAGR; competitor benchmarking table; Build-Buy-Hybrid strategic options; benefit/value-creation logic chains; financial projection (NPV, IRR, payback); risk–mitigation matrix by pillar; key assumptions. **No marketing frameworks** (no STP, funnel, campaign or marketing KPIs). Segmentation appears only as a list of target PPA buyers.

## How numbers are handled
- Industry stats without inline sources (23.9 GW potential, 2.4 GW installed, demand +4–5%/yr). The market table has no source line.
- **Financial headline:** IRR 58%, NPV "573" (no unit), payback 2 years, CAPEX US$12M. Quarterly bar charts are labelled "in million rupiah" with no totals, and the model is not shown.
- **Internal inconsistencies a judge could catch:**
  - The market table shows installed capacity 2.4 → 3.5 GW with "CAGR 65%". That is roughly +46% total over 6 years, about 6.5% CAGR.
  - The geothermal share of the energy mix is shown as 52% → 78%, which is implausible.
  - The problem headline calls PT DEL "perusahaan energi yang bergantung pada sumber fosil" (fossil-dependent), which contradicts it being a geothermal IPP.
  - The units don't reconcile: a US$12M capex (≈Rp190B) cannot pay back in 2 years on quarterly revenue bars of about Rp300M.
- No sensitivity analysis, no budget breakdown, no KPI targets, no timeline/roadmap slide (the "Implementation" tab exists in the nav but has no dedicated slide).

## Strengths
- Clear one-page **KQ → problem → root cause → solution map** with a priority order, which is easy for judges to follow.
- A crisp acronym (CAP) that maps 1:1 to the three problems; the solution reveal slide is visually distinct.
- Covers operational, commercial and financial-risk angles, each with build/buy/hybrid logic.
- The competitor table with emission factors and carbon strategies is a relevant, industry-specific benchmark.
- Risks are tied to specific pillars with concrete mitigations (fixed-price EPC with liquidated damages, 70% hedge via forward contracts, 3+ counterparties).

## Weaknesses / what a judge would criticize
- **Unfinished deck:** placeholder headers, an outline-as-exec-summary and typos signal poor QA. This is likely a key reason it did not advance past the semifinal.
- Numbers are unsourced and internally inconsistent (the CAGR, the energy-mix share, NPV without units, payback that is implausible against capex).
- No implementation roadmap, owners, budget breakdown or KPI targets, even though the nav promises "IMPLEMENTATION".
- No sensitivity or scenario analysis; financial assumptions are qualitative statements.
- Mixed Indonesian/English and justified small body text hurt readability.
- No closing recommendation or "ask"; the conclusion restates rather than decides.

## Reusable patterns
- **4-column problem map slide:** Key Question | Problem (3) | Root Cause (2 each) | Solution letters with 3 sub-initiatives each, plus a "Problem Priority 1→2→3" strip underneath.
- **Acronym reveal slide:** a full-bleed faded photo of the client's asset, the acronym letters in an accent colour + the client name in the primary colour, 3 white rounded cards with an icon on top, and the first letter enlarged in each card title.
- **Strategic-mode labels** per pillar (BUILD / BUY / HYBRID), a quick way to show option thinking.
- **Risk table grouped by pillar:** Strategy → 2 risks → mitigation each, with a key-assumptions column beside it.
- **Icon-circle benefit chain** (5 icons joined by a line, label + 2-line explanation), with a "Konklusi:" strip below each deep-dive.
- **Lessons (anti-patterns):** run a placeholder/typo sweep before export; put a source line on every table; check CAGR and units with code; always include roadmap + budget + KPIs + an ask; keep one language.
