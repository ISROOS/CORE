# [SemiFinal] BPC – AIESEC BINUS ("Lead The Change") — Sleep4win: Circular Electronics Recovery & E-Waste Management

## Competition & type
- **Type:** Business Plan Competition (AIESEC in BINUS; the "Lead The Change" logo sits top-right on every slide). This round's deliverable was a **Business Model Canvas** submission.
- **Result:** **Semifinal** (advanced past the preliminary round).
- **Team title:** "Sleep4win". **Work title:** "Circular Electronics Recovery & E-Waste Management".
- **Idea:** A B2B e-waste company. It collects obsolete corporate devices, performs certified data sanitisation/destruction, dismantles and tests components, resells graded parts to repair shops (Mangga Dua, Glodok, Bandung, Surabaya), and sends the remainder to licensed recyclers (EwasteRJ). It also provides ESG/sustainability reports to clients.

## Document type & format
- `BMC_Sleep4win.pdf`: **6 slides**, landscape 16:9 (1440×810 pt), 5.2 MB.
- Fonts: **Lato** (Bold/Regular) only.
- Text layer: live on the description and BMC slides; attachments are screenshots plus URLs.

## Storyline / argument pattern
- **Compliance-driven B2B pain:** corporations must dispose of devices safely (data-protection law, environmental sanctions, ESG/OJK sustainability reporting).
- **Answer:** a "one-stop" service bundling **secure data destruction + recovery + responsible disposal + ESG reporting**.
- **Two-sided value:** corporations pay for destruction; technicians buy cheap graded parts; the environment gets a longer component lifecycle (SDG 12).
- **Staged go-to-market:** *Initial stage* (laptop leasing/DaaS firms, OJK-regulated companies, data centres, telcos) → *Next stage* (data centres, telcos) → *Public sector* (data-erasure-only service; hardware follows government auction rules).
- The evidence attachments carry the "why now" (regulation + volume) instead of a narrative.

## Frameworks & analyses used
Business Model Canvas only (enhanced), plus a regulatory/evidence appendix. Notable BMC enrichments:
- **Key Partners** sub-grouped by role: Scrap Material Disposal (primary + backup licensed recycler + battery handler), Work Sources (leasing, logistics), Buyers, Supporting Parties (cyber insurance, environmental assurance firm).
- **Customer Segments** staged (Initial / Component Buyers / Next / Public Sector).
- **Value Propositions** split by beneficiary (Corporations / Technicians & Users / Environment).
- **Cost Structure** split Fixed vs Variable.
- **Revenue Streams** holds both revenue and cost bullets plus a profit-margin rule.

## How numbers are handled
- Very few numbers; this is a canvas round.
- **Pricing:** data destruction **Rp60,000–450,000 per device** depending on device and method; **on-site destruction +Rp150,000/device**; parts sold graded and warranted.
- **Margin rule:** "50% of gross profit from data destruction".
- **Market evidence** only through the attachment (1.9 M ton national e-waste, Komdigi). No TAM/SAM/SOM or projections.
- **Certifications as credibility assets:** ISO 27001 appears in both key resources and fixed costs; R2v3 and ISO 14001 appear in the attachments.

## Strengths
- A **sharp B2B wedge**: regulation-driven willingness to pay (data protection plus environmental compliance plus ESG reporting) rather than consumer goodwill.
- A **dual revenue model** (service fee per device + parts resale) with a concrete price range and an on-site upsell.
- Thoughtful partner design (backup recycler, battery specialist, cyber insurance, assurance firm), which shows operational risk awareness.
- Staged segments make the go-to-market believable.
- The evidence appendix ties claims to real Indonesian regulations and news.

## Weaknesses / what a judge would criticise
- The team name "Sleep4win" is unrelated to the business. It has no brand or product name and no tagline.
- No market size, volume or financial projection. The margin rule ("50% of gross profit from data destruction") is ambiguous.
- Cost lines inside the Revenue Streams block blur the BMC logic.
- Attachments are raw screenshots, not synthesised. Key facts aren't pulled out as callouts.
- Small text and dense bullets on a dark background hurt readability. The title slide uses a full slide for decoration.

## Reusable patterns
- **Sub-group BMC blocks** by role/stage (e.g. Partners: Disposal / Sources / Buyers / Supporting; Segments: Initial / Next / Public) to show strategic thinking inside a fixed template.
- **Split Value Propositions by beneficiary** (customer / secondary user / environment).
- Put **per-unit pricing with ranges and an upsell** straight into Revenue Streams.
- List **certifications** (ISO 27001, R2v3, ISO 14001) as both Key Resources and Fixed Costs. It signals credibility and realism.
- An **"Attachment" evidence slide**: screenshot + URL per claim. Improve it by adding a one-line highlighted takeaway per card.
- Dark navy + gold header-tab template: a fast, professional BMC look that fits "Lead The Change"-style branding.
