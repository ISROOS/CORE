## Storyline pattern
- **Hook:** a **shock statistic page before the cover** (3.1M deaths/yr from particulate pollution), then the cover. Problem-first, emotional, health-framed.
- **Answer first:** slide 1 is a **one-page exec summary** that already shows the problems, the question, the strategy names and the 3 return metrics (Pyramid principle).
- **Chain:** macro (Indonesia gold + ESG gap) → company problem (1.5M t uncovered tailings → PM10 → health) → **root cause tree** → **gap analysis** → PESTLE → "before" scene → Triple C overview → 3 deep dives (each Problem → Solution → Steps → Impact) → timeline + financials → risk → conclusion "after" scene (before/after framing).
- **Solution naming:** **Triple C Strategy**, an alliterative brand where each C is a 3-word "X to Y" phrase: **Cover to Recover / Collect to Convert / Community to Circularity**. Each phrase is a mini-story of transformation (it rhymes or alliterates), and the names map to environment → economy → community (the ESG triad).
- **Impact:** each deep dive ends in "Dampak" cards with cited % effects; the conclusion revisits the 3 problems as resolved (check-marks).

## Frameworks & analyses
Executive summary spine, root cause tree, **gap analysis (ideal/current/gap)**, PESTLE, ESG lens, SCQA (appx), SWOT-TOWS (appx), material comparison matrix (HDPE/PVC/LLDPE), material-flow allocation (50/50), process steps, precedent/benchmark cases ("Company Stories", "Implementasi Serupa"), off-take agreement design, 5-year swimlane roadmap, 5×5 risk heat map, cash-flow model, ROI/IRR/NPV/PBP.

## Numbers
- **Problem sizing:** 1.5M t dry tailings over 2 years, piles up to 15 m; PM10 >150 µg/m³ vs WHO 15; tailings = 39.2% of PM10 in residential areas; 3.1M deaths/yr (global).
- **Solution effects (cited):** dust suppressant −95% PM10/PM2.5 (Park 2019), HDPE cover −24.18% PM10 (Traven 2024), vegetation −26% PM10 (AQEC 2018); HDPE life 20–30 yrs exposed; tailings cut building material cost up to 50%.
- **Program targets:** 50% of tailings → building materials, 50% → dry stack; >15 BUMDes, >30 UMK; ≤10% volume incentive to BUMDes; Goldman off-takes ~40% of UMK output.
- **Market context:** green building materials CAGR 10.89% (2025–34); Sulawesi UMK producing paving/batako: Sulsel 10,536, Sultra 3,169, Sulteng 2,730, Sulbar 1,722, Sulut 1,557.
- **Financials (appendix, verified in Python):** outflow 2026–30 = Rp2.126B / 0.661B / 0.943B / 1.322B / 3.328B (Σ 8.38B; the biggest items are HDPE Rp1.4B in yr1 and off-take purchases ramping to Rp2.97B); inflow = 0 / 0.54B / 2.244B / 3.366B / 6.732B (Σ 12.88B; sales of dry tailings + UMK products). **ROI 53.7% = net 4.50B / 8.38B ✓**; **PBP 3.46 yrs ✓** (cumulative turns positive in yr 4); IRR recomputes to ~41.7% vs the stated 44.3% (close; convention difference); NPV ~2.5–2.7B at 10%, consistent with the "3 M" rounding. Key assumptions stated: 60,000 m² HDPE for 72,000 t in yr1, reused later; 50% dry-stacked; revenue growth driven by more UMK partners and longer off-take terms.
- **Research:** secondary only (no interviews/survey); about 25 references plus source URLs on every appendix evidence card.

## Why it likely won
- **Clean consulting logic** that judges can audit: exec summary → root-cause tree → gap analysis → each root cause mapped to a sub-initiative → impact → risk.
- **Memorable, logical naming** (Triple C, "X to Y" alliteration) covering E, S and the economic angle, which fits a "youth leader / ESG" competition.
- **Feasibility via precedents** at almost every step (PT NHM dry stack, Freeport/Antam paving blocks, PLN FABA × BUMDes, Pupuk Kaltim off-taker) and named partners (Sucofindo lab, BUMDes).
- **Numbers that reconcile** (ROI and PBP verified from the cash-flow table) with stated assumptions.
- **Community mechanism with teeth:** off-take agreement + training + distribution logistics + monitoring loop, beyond just "empower UMKM".

## Weaknesses
- The financial model is small (Rp8.4B spend over 5 yrs for 1.5M t) and **revenue logic is thin**: unit price/volume of tailings and UMK products sold is not shown; the off-take purchase appears as a cost and resale as revenue without a margin table. There is no sensitivity analysis.
- Minor inconsistencies: ">15 BUMDes" tile vs ">10 BUMDes" text; IRR 44.3% vs ~41.7% recomputed; "NPV 3 M" with no discount rate stated.
- No primary research or community voice despite a community-centred solution; social-license risk (residents sealing mines, CNBC 2025 is cited in the refs) is not quantified.
- The SCQA appendix is an image-only page; the hook stat is global and not local to Goldman's site.
- There is no explicit ask / approval decision or stage gate (an MVP "uji penerapan awal" exists in the timeline, but no go/no-go criteria).

## Differences vs Wijaya Team house style
**They do, Wijaya usually doesn't:**
- **Gap analysis table (Aspect × Ideal × Current × Gap)** as a dedicated slide between root cause and solution.
- **Alliterative "X to Y" pillar names** instead of a single-letter acronym (Triple C = 3 pillars, each a transformation phrase).
- **Before/after scenes:** an illustrated "Before Implementation" map with problem callouts mirrored by a "Conclusion" path with check-marks.
- **Precedent cards on every deep dive** ("Company Stories", "Implementasi Serupa") as feasibility proof.
- **"Tahukah anda?" cited micro-fact cards** inside the impact zone.
- **Material-flow diagram with % allocation** (50/50 split) to show where the physical waste goes.
- **Off-take agreement design** (share purchased, term, quality conditions) as the concrete community-economics mechanism.

**Wijaya does, they don't:**
- A persona/human hook that recurs (Triplecheese uses a stat-photo hook once).
- Scenarios/sensitivity, a break-even point and an assumption register beyond 3 bullets.
- Pilot + stage gates + capital-at-risk ask.
- Guardrails slide; appendix numbers (A1…) cited from the main slides (they use "*Appendix Supporting Data 2/3" footnotes, which is close).

**Shared:** issue chain/root-cause → pillars, one-page exec summary, bottom section tracker, action subtitle, sponsor-colored palette, an appendix with SWOT/TOWS and supporting data.

## Reusable techniques
- **Stat-shock page before the cover:** one giant red number + one-line claim + citation, over a photo of the problem.
- **Exec summary as a 4-node spine** (Problem → Question → Strategy → Impact) ending in 3 return metrics, as slide 1.
- **Root-cause tree whose last column is your solution components**, so every sub-initiative visibly traces back to a root cause.
- **Gap analysis slide** (5 aspects × ideal / current / gap with »» arrows) to quantify the distance to standard (e.g., PM10 15 vs >150).
- **Fixed deep-dive template:** Problem (⚠ header) | Why this option? | Solution | numbered Steps | Impact with "Tahukah anda?" cited facts.
- **"X to Y" alliterative pillar names** grouped under one umbrella (Triple C) as an alternative to letter acronyms.
- **Precedent card per pillar** (who did it, where, result) to pre-empt feasibility questions.
- **Swimlane timeline per pillar with a Planning/MVP/Improvement/Evaluation legend** + return metrics on the same slide.
- **Off-take mechanism** (buy X% of partner output, term, quality terms, then 3rd-party absorbers) for any UMKM-empowerment pillar.
- **Before-scene / after-path bookends** to make the impact story visual.
