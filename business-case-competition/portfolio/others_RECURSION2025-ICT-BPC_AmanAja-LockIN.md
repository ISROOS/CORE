## Storyline pattern
- **Hook:** a sharp **insight-reframe**. Addiction "isn't the user's negligence". Platforms are designed for compulsion, and software blockers fail because users simply switch them off. So the solution adds **physical friction**. This is the core "why we win" logic.
- **Problem → root cause → solution → impact:** the problem is social-media addiction (anxiety, sleep, productivity). The root cause is engineered instant gratification plus easily bypassed software limits. The solution is an NFC keychain + app (hardware friction, pause, mindful prompts). Impact is healthier digital habits, backed by a financial return.
- **Solution naming:** LockIN (a pun on "lock in" / focus, with "IN" as a padlock). The marketing acronym is **K.E.Y** (Kenalkan, Ekspos, Yakinkan), a pun that ties back to the physical key.

## Frameworks & analyses
STP (segmentation figure, targeting by size/profitability/accessibility, positioning map) · competitor feature matrix · TAM/SAM/SOM · K.E.Y marketing funnel (awareness → action → retention) · revenue model (subscription, hardware, bundle, family promo, rewarded ads, emergency unlock fee) · 5-yr cost & revenue projection · ROI / payback / NPV · 12-month Gantt · funding sources & allocation. **No user research, personas or usability testing.**

- **Context:** 48.6% of teens highly addicted (Muslim et al., 2024); productive-age population 69.58%; smartphone ownership by province (GoodStats).
- **Market:** TAM 150M urban (mostly Java) → SAM 104M productive-age → SOM 5M (≈5% of SAM). The derivation is mostly narrative, with no conversion maths.
- **Pricing:**
  - Basic Rp20k/month; Premium Rp100k/year.
  - Keychain only Rp60k.
  - Bundle (keychain + 1 yr) Rp139k.
  - Family −20% (min. 3 users).
  - Rewarded ads; emergency-unlock fee.
- **Financials:** year-1 cost Rp2.59B (digital marketing 801.9M, salaries 794.4M, NFC production + distribution 432.3M). Year-1 revenue Rp1.66B (bundle 764.5M, rewarded ads 396M), rising to Rp3.24B in year 5. ROI 30.99%, payback 3.8 yrs, NPV Rp1.863B.
- **Consistency check:** the chart's cumulative points are −0.93B (Y1), −0.69B (Y2), +0.11B (Y3), +1.56B (Y4), +3.04B (Y5). That implies payback ≈ **2.9 yrs**, not the stated 3.8, unless the 3.8 is discounted (not stated). The discount rate and ROI basis aren't shown. Rewarded-ad revenue of Rp396M in year 1 looks aggressive with no assumption shown.

## Why it likely won (BPC rubric: problem/insight, innovation, market, marketing, financial feasibility, presentation)
- **Genuinely differentiated insight** (physical friction beats software willpower) and a hardware+software moat vs one sec / ScreenZen.
- **Complete business-plan skeleton** (STP, TAM/SAM/SOM, pricing, marketing funnel, 5-yr projection, ROI/PBP/NPV, Gantt, funding) inside 19 body pages.
- **Memorable branding:** a strong logo/wordmark, tagline and K.E.Y pun, plus a premium dark cover that makes a student Word doc look like a startup deck.
- Multiple revenue streams that are easy to understand.

## Weaknesses
- **No primary research or validation** (no survey, interview or usability test), unusual even for an ICT BPC.
- **Payback inconsistency** (chart ≈2.9 yrs vs stated 3.8). No discount rate, no unit economics (COGS per keychain vs Rp60k price, CAC), no sensitivity analysis.
- SOM 5M with no conversion logic; revenue mix not reconciled to user counts.
- Times New Roman body clashes with the modern figure style. Long justified paragraphs.
- Typos ("Mencangkup", "fleksibiltas", "diakukan").

## Reusable techniques
- Lead the background with a **"it's not the user's fault, the system is designed this way" reframe**, then position the solution as an *external* intervention.
- Add a **physical touchpoint** (NFC tag, sticker, card) to an app idea for differentiation and a hardware revenue line.
- **Brand a plain document cheaply:** a corner logo tab, a footer tagline pill and a light watermark on every page.
- Show a **ROI / payback / NPV three-circle hero** right above the cash-flow chart. Make sure the chart and the circles agree.
- Draw **TAM/SAM/SOM as nested arcs** with a callout per level. Add the conversion maths (Wijaya rule).
- Make the **marketing-funnel acronym echo the product metaphor** (K.E.Y for a key-based product).
- Present **freemium + hardware + bundle + family plan** as a ladder (Rp60k → Rp139k bundle).
- Use a **12-month Gantt** for implementation in BPCs. It is quick and judges expect it.
