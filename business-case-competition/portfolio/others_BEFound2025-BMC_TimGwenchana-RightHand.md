## Storyline pattern
- **Hook:** a **photo comic before the cover**. The pitch opens in-story; the persona family (Anton/Sarah) and the ART (Rini) are introduced, and the product is shown solving the problem in panels 6–8. This is the same device as Wijaya's ITECHNO 🥇 / UBM 🥉 comic, but placed as page 1, before the title.
- **Chain:** facts (long work hours → high ART demand → trust/quality gap) → 3-card problem slide (big, emotional, photo-backed) → product (3 service types) → value prop acronym → market → competition → go-to-market → risk → impact → BMC → money → team.
- This is a classic **startup pitch sequence** (Problem → Solution → Market → Competition → GTM → Business model → Financials/Ask → Team), with the BMC kept as one slide near the end because the competition requires it.
- **Solution naming:** product brand "RightHand" (pun: right-hand person + hand logo) + two acronyms: **T.R.U.S.T** (features/value prop) and **C.A.R.E** (marketing). The acronyms carry the emotional message (trust, care), not just structure.
- **Ask:** "RightHand needs Rp 7.1 billion of funding in year 1 for app development, permits, marketing, operations, incentives and an emergency fund", with a use-of-funds pie (software & hardware 37.7%, marketing 33.6%, operations 12.9%, emergency 9.1%, permits 3.8%, incentives 2.8%).

## Frameworks & analyses
TAM/SAM/SOM, STP, 4P competitor matrix, perceptual positioning map, C.A.R.E funnel (≈ AARRR), **FMEA (Sev×Occ=RPN)**, SDG mapping, Business Model Canvas, NPV/ROI/PBP, use of funds.

## Numbers
- **Market:** TAM 153M (56.7% urban population), SAM 49M (17.37% middle/upper class), SOM 2.45M (5% of SAM). Units mix people and households (11.96M households are cited on p3), and the 5% capture is asserted, not derived.
- **Demand evidence:** avg 41 work hours/week (BPS 2025) vs 38.7 world (ILO 2025); ~5M active ARTs; 33.9% want ART training; 3 crime news cases (CNN, Detik) used as trust evidence.
- **Revenue model (from BMC):** 1% platform fee on contract value + Rp2,500 service fee per transaction + commission on ART earnings (part-time 10%, full-time 5%, ad hoc 10%) + in-app banner ads. Pricing has tiered Basic–Premium packages (no rupiah figures) and a Rp25k first-order discount.
- **Financials:** funding need Rp7.1B; NPV Rp9B; ROI 23.33%; PBP 1 year 1 month; 5-year line chart from ~Rp7B to ~Rp20B (axis unlabelled, so it is unclear whether this is revenue or GMV).
- **KPIs / traction / validation:** **none.** There is no survey, interviews, waitlist, LOIs, pilot, or unit economics (CAC, LTV, take rate per order, GMV per user).
- **Sources:** 8 references (BPS, ILO, GoodStats, Kompas, CNN, Detik), with citations in-slide.

## Visual design system
- **Palette:** cyan **#0CB4D8** + hot pink **#FC4884** (brand duo, also the 2-color wordmark "Right" cyan / "Hand" pink), accent marigold **#F0A818** (part-time bubble, user-customization), soft pink **#FCB4CC**, mauve overlay **~#894E59** on problem photos, cream background **#FFFBF5/#FFFFF7**. Decorative quarter-circle blobs (pale pink/pale cyan) in the corners, and soap-bubble circles.
- **Type:** Poppins Bold titles with **two-color title words** ("Pada **Faktanya**…", "Profil **Anggota**", "Terima **Kasih**"); pink pill highlight on a key word ("Apa **MASALAH**-nya?"); giant first letter for acronym items (T.R.U.S.T); comic font in speech bubbles.
- **Layout:** no fixed chrome (no tracker bar, no page numbers, no action titles). Each slide is a free poster composition. Titles are short labels, not sentences.
- **Components:** dashed pink-outlined cards with number badges (facts slide); tall rounded photo cards with tinted overlay; colored circle "bubbles" with flat character illustrations; **phone mockups** of the actual app UI (5 screens); emoji bullets in the BMC; numbers inside 3D glossy bubbles (ROI/PBP/NPV); cut-out photo people (cover, problem cards).
- **Charts:** donut, arc TAM diagram, pie (use of funds), line chart, 2×2 positioning map, tables (4P, FMEA with black severity cells and red-outlined RPN).
- **Illustration style:** a mix of real stock photos (comic, problem cards, STP tiles), flat vector characters (service types), app UI mockups and a 3D blue mascot (p6). Playful, consumer-brand feel matching the "crazy ideas" theme.
- **Density:** low to medium on the story slides, very high on the BMC (p12) and the 4P/FMEA tables.

## Why it likely won
- **Memorable, emotional opening** (comic) that makes a mundane domestic-help marketplace relatable; judges remember Anton/Sarah/Rini.
- **Real product craft:** 5 designed app screens show the solution exists as a UX, not just as an idea (the team has a UI/UX background).
- **Trust is the core insight**, and everything is branded around it: crime-news evidence → T.R.U.S.T features (verification, guarantee, 24/7 hotline) → positioning map axis "Comprehensive Trust System" → C.A.R.E "Education & Trust Building".
- **Covers every BMC rubric box** (9-block canvas + market + competition + marketing + risk + financials + social impact + team) in only 15 slides.
- A strong brand identity (2-color wordmark, consistent palette) fits a "beyond the ordinary" theme.

## Weaknesses
- **No validation/traction**: zero primary research despite a trust-heavy marketplace thesis.
- **Financials are thin and inconsistent**: ROI 23.33% vs payback in 13 months doesn't square (an asset that pays back in about a year should show a much higher multi-year ROI). The line chart has no metric label, and there are no revenue drivers, unit economics or P&L. "M" is used for miliar, which is ambiguous for English readers.
- Market sizing mixes individuals and households; SOM 2.45M (5%) is not derived.
- **Ethical/legal gaps**: taking up to 10% commission from domestic workers' pay and live-in workers' contracts. The ART protection bill (RUU PPRT) and the BP2MI/Kemnaker agency licensing regime are not discussed, and the 2-sided supply side (ART recruitment and retention economics) is thin.
- Too many fonts (7); the BMC slide is a wall of emoji text; there are no slide numbers.

## Differences vs Wijaya Team house style
**They do, Wijaya usually doesn't:**
- **Comic as page 1, before the cover.** Wijaya puts its comic inside the deck.
- **App UI mockups as the value-prop slide** (one screen per acronym letter). Wijaya did phone mockups at ITECHNO, but not in BPC/BMC decks.
- **Two acronyms** (product value prop + marketing plan) layered as sub-brands.
- **FMEA with RPN scoring** instead of a likelihood×impact matrix.
- **Perceptual positioning map** (2×2 with competitor logos).
- **Use-of-funds pie with an explicit funding amount** (a startup-style ask).
- A playful consumer brand look: free-form poster slides, no consulting chrome, bilingual quote close.

**Wijaya does, they don't:**
- Issue chain with root causes (RightHand goes facts → problems → product with no root-cause layer).
- Formulas, assumption tags, scenarios/sensitivity and a break-even point.
- Pilot + stage gates.
- Full-sentence action titles + a bottom tracker + an appendix.
- Proof/validation metrics (ITECHNO's SUS scores).

## Reusable techniques
- **Open with a 6–8 panel photo comic before the cover** that introduces a named persona family + the product's hero (the service provider) and ends with the product solving the scene.
- **One phone mockup per acronym letter** for the value proposition: the letter is the headline and the screen is the proof.
- **Problem slide as 3 tall photo cards** with a tinted brand overlay and a 3–4-word headline each, emotional and readable from the back of the room.
- **Make the core insight a positioning-map axis** (e.g., "Comprehensive vs Minimal Trust System") so the competitor slide restates your thesis.
- **FMEA table** (Severity, Occurrence, RPN, cause, effect, mitigation) as a more quantitative risk slide; highlight RPN cells.
- **Two-color title words + pill highlight on the key noun** as a cheap, consistent brand device.
- **Ask slide pattern:** funding amount sentence + use-of-funds pie + 3 return bubbles (ROI/PBP/NPV) + a 5-year trend line, all on one slide. (Improve it by labelling the metric and adding the unit-economics drivers.)
- **Team slide with credential bullets** (competition count, roles) to signal execution capability.
