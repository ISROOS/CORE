## Competition / type / round / placement
- **Competition:** International Business Case Competition (IBCC), BRAND UI 2025. The case owner is Beiersdorf Indonesia (NIVEA body lotion). The cover shows the BRAND UI and Beiersdorf logos and reads "Proposal for International Business Case Competition BRAND UI 2025".
- **Type:** brand/marketing case. The task is to revive NIVEA body lotion's relevance and share among Gen Z.
- **Round:** written proposal round (likely the preliminary/paper stage).
- **Placement:** winner per user; the placement is not stated in the document and there is no certificate.
- **Team:** 3 people (Sabrina, Elizabeth C.B., Dave R.).

## Format
- 36 pages, A4 portrait (595×842 pt).
- The **main body is 14 numbered pages** (Executive Summary through Conclusion). The rest is front matter (cover and a 2-page TOC), 1 page of references and 18 pages of appendices (Appendix 1–12).
- It is built as an **academic-paper Word document**: Times New Roman body text, justified, roughly 1.5 line spacing, numbered chapters, "Fig. n. Title (Source: Team Analysis)" captions and APA references. All diagrams are image figures made in Canva.

## Storyline pattern
- **Hook:** category growth versus brand stagnation. The body-lotion market is growing, 71% of sales are brightening products and Vaseline and Citra own "glow", while NIVEA's protection positioning "lags in relevance" with Gen Z. The hook is market-led; there is no persona.
- **Problem → root cause:**
  - The key question is "How can NIVEA increase market penetration and share of its body lotion portfolio … among Gen Z?"
  - It leads to 3 issues: product-market misalignment, low emotional resonance in communication, and channel visibility and conversion gaps.
  - Each issue has sub-issues and root causes, e.g. low UV literacy (SPF vs UV filter), an informative rather than purpose-driven voice, and a shelf that blends with Vaseline.
  - The exec summary condenses this to 3 challenges: the UV-literacy gap, communication limits and channel visibility.
- **Solution:** the big idea is "**Confidence Starts NOW**", an acronym campaign platform: **N**urture Authentic Stories / **O**pen Real Understanding / **W**eave Lifestyle Experience. Each pillar is a page with a giant drop-cap initial in its header.
  - **N (digital):** a #ShineNOW TikTok Branded Mission with creator codes linked to a #NIVizen loyalty programme; "Emotional Storytelling through Education" turning "How-to" into "Why-to" via a 3-act creator script (Vulnerability → Ritual → Confidence).
  - **O (in-store):** a QR-coded Smart Shelf Education display; an Interactive Try-Me Zone; a Skin-Check Corner with skin analysis; key retail partners Guardian, Watsons, Sociolla and others.
  - **W (experiential/product):** gamified Experience Pop-Up Booths with stamps; **NIVEA On-The-Go** (a mini lotion with a carabiner and refills); NIVEA RUN 2.0 (a fun-run).
- **Impact:** the positioning shift is "functional lotion" → "confidence and care companion". The headline financials are ROI 22.9%, payback 2.4 years, NPV ≈Rp1.42B and +89.3% sales by 2028.
- **Solution naming:** a tagline that doubles as an acronym (NOW), with brand-native hashtags (#ShineNOW, #NIVizen, NIVEA RUN 2.0).

## Frameworks & analyses
- Root-cause tree (Key Q → Issue → Sub-issue → Root cause)
- Primary research: an online survey plus field observation with shelf photos as evidence
- Secondary literature synthesis (about 10 journal citations)
- STP, TAM/SAM/SOM, a competitor comparison table, SWOT/TOWS (SO/WO strategies drive the solution), 4P, and 5C (appendix)
- Solution matrix with rows Objective / What / Problem / Details / Why
- Risk register plus an impact×severity matrix (5 risks)
- Milestone timeline and Gantt chart
- Cash-flow statement with ROI, NPV and payback
- Before/after impact diagram
- No hypothesis testing, no sensitivity analysis and no media/budget allocation chart.

## Numbers
- **Research:** online survey of **n=68** (61 lotion users), mostly Gen Z/young millennial women. Headline stats:
  - 91.8% value UV protection, but only 31.1% understand SPF vs UV filter
  - 62.3% see NIVEA as trusted
  - TikTok is the main discovery platform (68.9%)
  - Content preferences: educational 44.3%, story-driven 37.7%
  - Interest in samples 67.2% and testing 63.9%
- **Field study:** 4 retail chains (Guardian, Watsons, Century, Sociolla).
- **Market sizing:** TAM US$819.95m (Indonesia body care 2025, Statista, 5.55% CAGR) → SAM US$459.17m (56% body-lotion share) → SOM US$19.52m (NIVEA's 4.25% share, Markethac). The arithmetic is consistent, although the SOM is "current share", not a target.
- **Pillar KPIs** (impact strip of 4 chips per pillar, with no baselines or formulas):
  - N: UGC +41%, cost efficiency +28%, engagement +35%, loyalty conversion +24%
  - O: product understanding +34%, trial-to-purchase +28%, shelf engagement +19%, consultation time −25%
  - W: lifestyle affinity +31%, community engagement +26%, repeat purchase +22%, eco perception +18%
- **Before/after impact:**
  - TikTok marketscope ~2.25% → >10%
  - Body-lotion market share 5% → >15%
  - Shelf visibility ~10% → >30%
  - UV literacy rising to ~70%
- **Financials:**
  - Quarterly cash flow 2026–2028. Revenue lines: Beiersdorf initial subsidy Rp750M, booth/roadshow revenue, On-The-Go sales and refills, body-lotion income, NIVEA Run fees, recycling partner income, sponsorship.
  - Expense lines: salary and KOL, TikTok mission, packaging, events, roadshow logistics, smart shelves (500+ stores), Skin-Check Corner (25 outlets in Greater Jakarta), run expenses, CapEx, a contingency line.
  - Results: ROI 22.9%, NPV ≈Rp1.42B, payback ≈2.44 years.
- **Sources:** Statista, Markethac, the case book and Indonesian journals 2022–25.

## Why it likely won (vs a typical brand-case rubric: insight, analysis, creativity, feasibility, financial impact, presentation)
1. **A clear insight that ties to the product's real USP.** The UV-literacy gap is evidenced by both survey and literature, and NIVEA's UV-filter lotion answers it. That makes "education" a legitimate brand role, not a gimmick.
2. **Integrated IMC across the funnel.** Digital (TikTok mission plus a storytelling script), in-store (smart shelf, try-me, skin check), experiential (pop-ups, Run) and product (On-The-Go with refills) all feed one platform name. The executions are tangible, shown as mockups, a storyboard and booth renders.
3. **Field evidence.** Shelf photos show NIVEA blending with Vaseline. It is a cheap and persuasive proof point.
4. **The template is followed exactly**, with every expected framework present (STP, sizing, competitor, SWOT/TOWS, 4P, 5C, risk, timeline, cash flow, NPV/ROI).
5. **The brand look is spot-on.** The NIVEA navy palette and polished pillar pages read like an agency deck inside an academic paper.

## Weaknesses
- **Thin, unanchored KPIs.** The +41% and +28% figures have no baseline, formula or measurement method, and "market share 5% → >15%" conflicts with the stated 4.25% SOM.
- **Questionable financial methodology.** The "Beiersdorf initial subsidy" is booked as *revenue*, and the cash-flow statement is an unreadable screenshot. The NPV in the text is Rp1,417,659,988, while the table appears to read ≈Rp1,471,659,788 (read via vision; a possible digit transposition). A 22.9% ROI over 2 years is modest for that spend.
- Small sample (n=68), skewed female and young, with no significance testing. The field study covered only 4 stores.
- There is no persona or human story, so the hook is generic market data.
- The exec summary is prose only, with no visual one-pager.
- Several things are missing: a media plan or budget split by channel, a funnel or conversion model, and measurement tooling (brand-lift or awareness tracking).
- Typos: "Souce", "Implemetation", "sslifestyle".

## Differences vs Wijaya Team house style
**They do, Wijaya usually doesn't:**
- Write in academic-paper form (Word, Times New Roman, numbered chapters, "Fig. n (Source: Team Analysis)", APA references) and drop in designed Canva figures. Wijaya builds designed HTML/deck pages throughout.
- Show **creative executions** as tangible artefacts: a 3-act creator script plus storyboard, a TikTok mission mechanic, booth and shelf renders, and a new SKU (NIVEA On-The-Go with packaging spec). Wijaya's marketing entries (Blibli, FIT) focus on mechanism and numbers rather than creative.
- Use **field-observation photo evidence** (shelf audits).
- Give each pillar page a giant drop-cap header and a 4-chip "Impact" bar.

**Wijaya does, they don't:**
- A persona hook and a recurring human story.
- Formula-backed numbers with downside/base/upside cases, sensitivity analysis and an assumption register.
- Stage-gated pilot plus capital-at-risk and an explicit ask.
- A "what we won't do" guardrails slide.
- A one-page infographic exec summary.
- A "Takeaway:" line on appendix pages.
- The pillar × root cause × KPI × budget matrix. TSS maps pillars to problems but not to budget lines.

**Lesson:** Wijaya's financial rigour would have beaten this entry on numbers. TSS won on **insight-to-execution coherence and brand-faithful creative**, which is Wijaya's gap in brand cases.

## Reusable techniques
- Use a **campaign tagline that is also the acronym** ("Confidence Starts NOW" → N/O/W), and put the giant initial in each pillar header.
- Find a **"literacy gap" insight** (people value X but don't understand it), let the brand's own technical USP close it, and make "education" a pillar.
- Map the IMC as **digital × in-store × experiential × product**, with one named programme per channel and brand-coined hashtags.
- Use a **3-act creator brief** (Vulnerability → Ritual → Confidence/"NOW moment") to show judges exactly what the content looks like. It turns "How-to" content into "Why-to".
- Run a **shelf audit with photos** across 3–4 retailers, compare the weak retailer against the best one (Sociolla as benchmark), and propose a smart shelf with QR.
- Close every pillar page with an **Impact bar of 4 KPI chips**. Upgrade it Wijaya-style with a baseline → target and a formula.
- Show a **before/after implementation impact** diagram as the closing appendix.
- Mark the priority initiatives on the timeline with "PRIORITY" tags and photo circles.
