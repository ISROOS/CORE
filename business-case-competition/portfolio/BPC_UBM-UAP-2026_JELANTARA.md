## Storyline / argument pattern
1. **Emotional hook first.** A comic storyboard shows the pain (no easy disposal) and resolves it with the product before any data appears.
2. **Problem quantified, with a reframe.** "Masalah besar, nilai yang terbuang": waste framed as lost value. The punchline names the root cause: it's not supply, it's the *lack of an integrated collection system*.
3. **Solution as an ecosystem.** Four named components (S-M-A-C), then a step-by-step workflow, then the business model.
4. **Proof of fit.** SWOT, TAM/SAM/SOM + STP, and a competitor matrix that ends in a positioning claim ("the only one with complete operations").
5. **Go-to-market.** The G.R.E.E.N acrostic in the deck; the AARA funnel in the proposal.
6. **Feasibility.** Capex → KPIs → 5-year profit, then a 12-month roadmap, then demos (UI flow, how-to).
7. **Branding devices:** a portmanteau name (JELANTAhRA → "JELANTARA", from jelantah + "antara/nusantara"); a tagline ("Jelantah Jadi Rupiah", later "Jelantah Terkelola, Bumi Terjaga"); a slogan footer ("From Waste to Worth, Together for a Greener Future"); and acronym frameworks (S-M-A-C, G.R.E.E.N).

## How numbers are handled
- **Market sizing (top-down, volume × price):**
  - TAM: 2–3 bn L/yr = Rp16–24 T, i.e. roughly Rp8,000/L.
  - SAM (urban): 600–900 M L = Rp4.8–7.2 T.
  - SOM: 3–4.5 M L (0.5% of SAM) = Rp12–18 bn.
  - The deck labels these "juta TON / ribu Ton" while the proposal says "liter". The units are inconsistent.
- **Unit economics:** buy B2C ≈ Rp3,500/L (+ Green Reward points), buy B2B ≈ Rp4,500/L, sell to industry ≈ Rp9,000/L. Gross spread ≈ Rp4,500–5,500/L. Prices are triangulated from market sources (Pertamina UCollect, MIJEL, JualJelantah.com).
- **Revenue streams, Y1 → Y5 (IDR):**
  - B2C oil 280 M → 1.56 bn
  - B2B oil 340 M → 2.18 bn
  - Merchant partnership 40 → 320 M
  - ESG dashboard subscription 30 → 520 M
  - Carbon credit 20 → 420 M
  - **Total 710 M → 5.0 bn**
- **Capex: Rp1.5 bn**
  - 7 smart machines × Rp100 M = 700 M
  - AI/ML 120 M; app 100 M; IoT 100 M
  - Office rent 120 M, renovation 70 M, equipment 55 M
  - Branding 100 M, legal/HKI 35 M, cloud 50 M, working capital 50 M
- **COGS:** green rewards, B2B oil purchase, transport, QC, storage. Rp460 M (Y1) → 1.775 bn (Y5).
- **Opex:** a Y1 base of Rp600 M (4 staff = 240 M), then a flat **+21%/yr** escalator.
- **P&L:** net profit −350 M, −91 M, +371.5 M, +1.03 bn, +1.94 bn.
- **KPIs (checked):**
  - PBP 4.28 yrs: cumulative profit after Y4 is 962 M; the remaining 538 M ÷ 1,939 M = 0.28.
  - "Average annual ROI" 38.69% = (Σ 5-yr net profit 2.90 bn ÷ 5) ÷ 1.5 bn.
  - NPV@10% = Rp294,539,527.
- **Citations:** source tags under every KPI card in the deck; in-text (Author, Year) citations in the proposal; APA bibliography with URLs; regulations cited (UU 32/2009, Permen ESDM 24/2021).

## Strengths
- A clear **problem → value-leak → root cause** framing backed by government and NGO data, with sources visible on the slide.
- The **multi-stream model** (spread + merchant + ESG SaaS + carbon credit) makes the model look less dependent on a single stream; B2C and B2B are separated cleanly.
- **Financials are complete and internally consistent.** Capex is itemised, COGS is separate from opex, there is a P&L, and PBP/ROI/NPV reconcile with the tables.
- Operational depth (sensor-level design, return faucet for rejected oil, 80–90% fill-trigger logistics) signals feasibility.
- Memorable branding: tagline, acrostic frameworks, a consistent product render, and a comic opener.
- The one-page infographic exec summary in the proposal compresses the whole plan into 6 blocks.

## Weaknesses / what a judge would criticise
- **SOM vs revenue mismatch.**
  - SOM is claimed as 3–4.5 M L ≈ Rp12–18 bn/yr, but Y5 revenue is only Rp5 bn. Oil revenue of ~3.7 bn at Rp9,000/L ≈ 0.41 M L.
  - With only **7 machines** in capex and no capex for later expansion, the volume path to the SOM is unexplained.
- **Unit errors.** The deck uses "ton" where the proposal uses "liter" for the same TAM/SAM/SOM figures.
- **"BEP tahun ke-3" vs PBP 4.28 years** are presented side by side without explaining the difference (first profitable year vs capital payback).
- **Simplified finance.** No tax, depreciation, cash-flow statement or sensitivity analysis. Opex is a flat +21% escalator. ESG and carbon-credit revenue are speculative and lack assumptions (number of clients, tCO₂e, price/ton).
- **Low-quality appendix tables.** Tables are flattened images with small text; there are typos ("Smrt Collection", "STRENGTHSS", "menangkapm", "COst").
- **Slide order oddity.** The comic and fact slides come before the cover.
- **No quantified validation.** No survey, pilot, LOI or traction is shown.
- Heavy reliance on AI-generated renders could read as concept-only.

## Reusable patterns
- Open with a **6–8 panel comic storyboard** of the persona's pain → product → payoff, then hit with a **fact dashboard** whose cards each carry a source tag.
- Finish the problem slide with a **root-cause insight bar**: "The problem isn't X, it's Y."
- **Two-tone slide titles** (dark brand colour + accent on the key word) and logo top-right on every slide.
- Name solution components and the marketing plan with **acrostics** (S-M-A-C, G.R.E.E.N).
- **Unit-economics line** on the business model slide (buy price B2C / buy price B2B / sell price) so judges can see the spread.
- **3 KPI bubbles (ROI / PBP / NPV) + capex donut + profit line** on one financial slide; full capex/opex/COGS/P&L tables in an appendix slide.
- Proposal: an **infographic one-page exec summary** with 6 numbered blocks, Times New Roman body, and figures numbered "Gambar X.0" with details pushed to the appendix.
- Competitor **check/cross feature matrix** naming real incumbents (Pertamina UCollect etc.) + a 2×2 perceptual map placing yourself top-right.
- Show feasibility with **hardware-level process detail** and a **12-month, 6-phase Gantt** plus a milestone strip.
- **Fix before reuse:** align SOM ↔ revenue ↔ capex volume, add tax/depreciation/sensitivity, and keep units consistent.
