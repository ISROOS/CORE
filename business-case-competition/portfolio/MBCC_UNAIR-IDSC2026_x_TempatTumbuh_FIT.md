- **Result:** **[Final Top 4]** per the folder tag. The cover says "MBCC BUSINESS CASE · SEMIFINAL", so this is the semifinal deck that advanced the team to the top-4 final.

## Storyline / argument pattern
- **Hook headline:** "Awareness is rising. Action is not." This frames the brand mission (knowing → doing gap) with paired OJK stats against behaviour stats.
- Problem → root cause → three pillars, set up on p4 and then **paid off one letter per slide** (F → I → T), with a section-divider slide before the solution.
- **Branded acronym:** **FIT** = **F**ocus (own the first-salary moment), **I**gnite (measurable B2B2C funnel), **T**hrive (grow with the user). The letters follow a marketing logic chain: STP choice → acquisition → retention/LTV. "FIT" also puns on financial fitness and segment fit.
- **Moment-based positioning:** "Your first salary. Your first financial system." The target is defined by a life moment, not just a demographic. Indonesian-language campaign hooks are used for local resonance ("Gaji Pertama, Ngapain Dulu?", "Korban Gajian Boros", "Progres Tumbuhmu").
- Persona (Rangga, 23, startup staff in Jakarta) and a customer decision journey show where awareness, purchase and loyalty break.
- Every proposed element is labelled ("Proposed MVP", "Existing modules", "Proposed path"), separating reuse from new build. This fits the "lean 7-person team" constraint.
- Ends on the transformation flow plus KPI targets. There is no explicit funding "ask" slide.

## Frameworks & analyses used (marketing focus)
- **STP:** segmentation bases + 5-criteria targeting scorecard (market size, problem intensity, WTP, competition, mass-acquisition ease) + positioning statement + sharpened value proposition.
- **TAM/SAM/SOM** (concentric circles, flagged as estimates).
- **Customer decision journey** (Awareness → Purchase → Loyalty) with current/gap/future state; persona.
- **Competitor mapping** table + **2×2 perceptual map**; deeper feature benchmark in the appendix.
- **SWOT → TOWS.**
- **Funnel / B2B2C acquisition design:** partner access → free session → diagnostic → starter → paid upgrade, i.e. a See-Think-Do-Care / AARRR-style flow.
- **Value ladder / product ladder** with price points (LTV growth); retention mechanics (progress dashboard, social sharing, referral loop).
- **Campaign concepts:** onboarding session title, alumni UGC storytelling content series.
- **KPIs:** active partners, Starter conversion, referral sign-ups, 30-day retention (Year-1 targets); a primary KPI per stakeholder/pod.
- Roadmap with decision gates; stakeholder map; resources plan; org pods.
- **Finance:** CAPEX/OPEX/COGS, revenue build, quarterly cash flow, NPV @10%, ROI, payback, best/base/worst sensitivity.
- Risk list + P×I matrix.
- External validation via international benchmarks and academic citations (OECD, CFPB, Kaiser et al. 2022, Karlan et al. 2016).

## How numbers are handled
- **Sourced macro data:** OJK SNLIK 2025, GoodStats 2024, BPS Statistik Pemuda 2024, Komdigi citing APJII, Bank Indonesia LIKE IT! 2024, Deloitte Gen Z Survey 2026, EBRI 2025. Sources appear as small lines or chips per slide.
- **Market sizing:** TAM ±107M / SAM ±30–35M / SOM ±300–600K, given as ranges and labelled "estimates for strategic direction". **The derivation is not shown**, and the 107M TAM sits next to "64.22M Indonesian youth" without reconciliation.
- **Financials are small-scale and realistic for a 7-person startup:** Year-1 outflow Rp610M (CAPEX 70 / OPEX 400 / COGS 140), inflow Rp746M. Drivers: Rp8M revenue per partnership per quarter, 40 participants per partnership per quarter, 10% upgrade to the Rp300K class, 2% to the Rp750K clinic.
- **Cash flow:** −190, −28, +156, +198 → NCF +Rp136M; ROI 22.3%; NPV(10%) Rp112M; payback month 9.9; net cash margin 18.2%. The outflow is also cut by pillar (F 100 / I 360 / T 150), which links budget to strategy.
- **Sensitivity:** worst (20 partners, 8% conversion → −Rp63M, ROI −11%), base, best (45 partners, 16% → +Rp388M, ROI 55%). It is explicitly labelled "illustrative, directional".
- **Minor inconsistency:** the roadmap targets "≥23% Year-1 ROI" while the financial slide shows 22%.
- Segment scoring is qualitative (High/Medium/Low, with asterisks).

## Strengths
- The marketing logic is textbook-complete and tied together: **STP → journey → funnel → ladder → retention → KPIs → cash flow**, with each FIT letter owning a stage, a budget slice and a KPI.
- A clear, defensible **targeting decision** using a scored matrix and an explicit "DECISION" box, which answers the case's central question (segment priority) head-on.
- Moment-based positioning (first salary) + B2B2C channel (employer onboarding / campus career center) solves the "Instagram/WhatsApp-only acquisition" problem in a scalable way.
- Right-sized financials for the client (Rp hundreds of millions, not billions), with CAPEX/OPEX/COGS separation, NPV, payback and three scenarios.
- Strong evidence layer: academic and international benchmarks validate each pillar, and every proposed element is labelled proposed vs existing.
- A thorough appendix (curriculum blueprint, partner pipeline, org pods, risk matrix, app mockups) gives depth for Q&A.

## Weaknesses / what a judge would criticize
- TAM/SAM/SOM is asserted, not derived, and is inconsistent with the youth-population stat. The SOM range (300–600K) is never connected to the Year-1 participant volume, which is roughly 30 partners × 40 participants.
- Targeting scores are qualitative with asterisks; there is no weighting or primary data. WTP for the chosen segment is "Low–Medium", which the plan acknowledges only through risk mitigation.
- Revenue relies on partnership fees (Rp560M of Rp746M), but employer willingness to pay Rp8M/quarter is unvalidated.
- KPI targets (12% conversion, 50% retention) have no benchmark source shown on the slide.
- ROI mismatch (22% vs ≥23%), typos and "SEMIFINAL" labelling suggest rushed QA. The marketing budget split by channel (paid/owned/earned) and CAC are not shown.
- App mockups are dark-themed and visually inconsistent with the light-blue deck system.

## Reusable patterns
- **Cover:** acronym with letters in pillar colours ("**F**I**T** for <Client>") + a one-line promise + three "Letter — Verb: one-liner" columns, team headshots and partner logos.
- **Hook headline built as a contrast pair:** "Awareness is rising. Action is not.", backed by 2 rising stats vs 3 behaviour-gap stats.
- **Exec summary as 4 boxes:** Question / Challenges / Strategy (one sentence) / Impact (3 financial tiles).
- **STP slide:** S/T/P letter cards on the left, segmentation-base arrow bars below, concentric TAM/SAM/SOM on the right, target snapshot at the bottom.
- **Targeting scorecard:** criteria rows × segment columns → "PRIORITY #1/#2/#3", a navy "DECISION" callout and a sharpened value-proposition quote.
- **Journey slide:** Current State → Gap → Future State cards + persona card + a path with red ✕ at Awareness, Purchase and Loyalty breakpoints.
- **Funnel as step cards,** each with an evidence chip (source or "Proposed MVP" / "Existing modules"), so judges can see reuse vs new build.
- **Value ladder with price points** (Free → Rp150K → 300K → 750K → 1.5M+) to show the LTV path.
- **Local-language campaign names** for concepts ("Gaji Pertama, Ngapain Dulu?", "Progres Tumbuhmu") inside an English deck.
- **Roadmap grid:** columns = quarters with phase names (Validate → Pilot → Scale → Optimize); rows = quarter goal / each pillar / decision gate.
- **KPI band** repeated on the financial and conclusion slides (navy label + 4 big numbers).
- **Outflow split by pillar** in the cash-flow table (F/I/T rows) to link money to strategy.
- **Best/base/worst table** on the two main drivers, plus a "what moves the needle" sentence.
- **Appendix "proof" slides:** international benchmarks mapped to each pillar ("Validates I — Ignite"), stakeholder map with a primary KPI per party, resource plan with a "Year-1 principle" column, product mockups.
