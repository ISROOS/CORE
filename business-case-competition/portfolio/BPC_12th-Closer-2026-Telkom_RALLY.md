## Storyline / argument pattern
- **Insight-led framing:** "people want to do activities but have no one to do them with" + "friendship happens naturally through repeated shared activities". The activity becomes the social vehicle.
- **Two-sided marketplace:** Participants (Activity Seekers, Social Explorers) ↔ Hosts (monetise hobbies and skills).
- **VPC** maps each pain (no friends to go with, awkwardness joining alone, scattered info, host credibility) to a feature (discovery, booking, group chat, host verification, ratings, recurring activities).
- Ends on product proof (branding + UI mockups) rather than numbers.
- **Branding:** a short verb-name "RALLY" with the tagline "Do More Together".

## Frameworks & analyses used
Business Model Canvas · Value Proposition Canvas (jobs split into Functional/Social/Emotional) · brand identity sheet (logo + palette) · UI mockups.

## How numbers are handled
- Pricing only, with no market sizing or projections:
  - **15% commission** per activity transaction
  - **Host Premium Rp99,000/month**
  - Venue partnership revenue share
  - Brand collaboration / sponsorship
- **Costs** listed qualitatively: app development, cloud, marketing/UA, community and host ops, support, legal, partnerships, payment processing.
- No sources or citations; the problem is asserted, not evidenced.

## Visual design system
- **Palette:**
  - Lavender/lilac backgrounds `#BEADF8`, `#C0B0F0`, `#D0C0F0`
  - Deep purple `#524087`
  - Electric violet `#7D37FF`, periwinkle `#8B7BDA`
  - Cream cards; pink chrome title
  - The product brand palette is separate: royal blue `#2D5BE8`, lime `#C6E93F`, coral `#F97B67`, pale blue `#CFD6F7`, navy `#151A33`
- **Style:** Y2K / "chrome-3D" aesthetic (iridescent flowers, liquid metal, sparkles, script display fonts) for the shell. The product mockups use a clean modern app UI.
- **Layout:** BMC as rounded cards with pill headers; VPC as the standard square-circle diagram with lavender pill labels; full-bleed decorative divider.
- **Text density:** high and small on the BMC/VPC. The serif (Times) body makes canvases look dated against the trendy display type.
- **Recurring components:** purple pill headers, rounded cream cards, logo strip in Key Partners, sparkle decorations.

## Strengths
- A clear, relatable consumer insight with a two-sided marketplace logic (supply = hosts, demand = seekers).
- VPC with functional / social / emotional job breakdown shows empathy depth.
- A concrete monetisation mix: commission + SaaS-like host subscription + venue + brand deals.
- Shows a tangible **product identity** (logo, palette, UI) that makes the concept feel real.

## Weaknesses / what a judge would criticise
- **No market evidence:** no data on loneliness or social-activity demand, no TAM/SAM/SOM, no competitor analysis (Meetup, Bumble BFF, community IG accounts, sports-booking apps like AYO), no financials.
- A **cold-start / chicken-and-egg** risk isn't addressed. Safety and trust for strangers meeting offline is only implied (host verification).
- Brand-name **logos in Key Partners** (BCA, Nike, GoPay) imply partnerships that don't exist.
- The decorative 3D shell (and a full divider slide) consumes 2 of 5 slides. Times New Roman body clashes with the style. The file is 17 MB.
- The shell palette (lilac/pink) doesn't match the product brand palette (blue/lime/coral), so the identity is fragmented.
- Typos in the canvas ("aktivitiy", "Appartement", "mina").

## Reusable patterns
- Pair the **BMC with a VPC** when a round allows only canvases. It proves customer understanding beyond the template.
- Split customer jobs into **Functional / Social / Emotional** and map each pain to a named feature.
- Close with a **"Portofolio Bisnis" brand sheet**: logo, palette swatches labelled with hex codes, and 2 hero app screens with real-looking content (price, location, participants).
- Revenue mix for marketplaces: **take rate (%) + supplier subscription (Rp/month) + venue revenue share + brand sponsorship**.
- **Avoid next time:** decorative-only slides in short decks, serif body text on modern templates, and implied partner logos. Add 1–2 data points on the problem and a competitor row even inside a canvas round.
