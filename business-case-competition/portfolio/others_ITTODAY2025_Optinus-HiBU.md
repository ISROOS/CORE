## Storyline pattern
- **Hook:** an emotional photo cover plus an **invisible-burden narrative** ("mental load"). The background is told as a story about motherhood expectations vs reality, then made concrete with a stat (79% vs 37% of tasks).
- **Problem → root cause → solution → impact:**
  - Problem: hidden mental load leading to burnout or PPD.
  - Causes: no safe place to talk, stigma ("lebay", "malu ke psikolog"), no time for self, unreliable info.
  - Solution: LENTERA (7 features).
  - Impact: stakeholder outcomes with cited baseline figures.
- **Empathy research is the emotional core.** Empathy-map quotes are raw Indonesian stigma lines that make judges feel the problem.
- **Solution naming:**
  - Product HiBU ("Hi" + "Ibu").
  - Acronym **LENTERA** ("lantern", a meaningful Indonesian word, like SIAP): Lifestyle personalization, Education, Nurturing journal, Techno-AI companion, Expertise support, Relation community, Actionable gamification.
  - AI persona "Bibu".
  - Metaphor fit: a lantern for a dark period.

## Numbers
- **Context:** University of Bath (labelled "AS"): mothers do 79% of daily tasks vs fathers 37%, and 53% of occasional tasks. PPD 22.4%; 60% of young mothers stressed; 50–70% isolated post-partum; 675k–1M PPD cases/yr.
- **Survey:**
  - Top problems: no time for self 29.7%, managing emotions 24.3%, overwhelmed 21.6%, lonely 13.5%.
  - Needs: personal time 34.2%, trusted info 17.1%.
  - Wanted features: articles 30.6%, anonymous venting 19.4%.
  - Preferred usage style: "simple" 53.6%.
- **Usability:** a single round. SEQ mean 4.4 (per-mission bars ~3.3–5.0). SUS 89.17. **Tester count not stated**, and there is no before/after (the iteration is qualitative).
- **No market sizing, business model or financials.** Only a "consultation fee" appears in the rich picture.
- References: academic (BMC Psychiatry, J Pediatric Health Care, World Bank) with DOIs. This is the strongest literature base of the three.

## Visual design system
- **Palette:** blush #FDE4E7 / #FBF8F8 backgrounds, raspberry/rose #C32744 (stated primary), dusty mauve #9E505A / #A98C8D, greige #E6E5E4 + soft photo tints. Warm, feminine, calm, matched to the audience.
- **Type:**
  - Document: a **serif** display for section titles (large raspberry uppercase serif with drop-shadow, plus a vertical accent bar), serif body (Times-like) and a serif running header. This is unusual for UI/UX entries.
  - App: Manrope.
- **Grid/frame:** single column. Thin pink header rule with the competition name and footer rule with logo + pink page tab. Faded photo strips bleed off top/bottom of section-divider pages.
- **Components:** rounded blush cards with numbered red circles, stakeholder cards with emoji, survey pies, quote cards with portrait photos, hook-model canvas, persona cards, 6-zone empathy map, affinity columns, stacked Problem/HMW/LENTERA band (vertical red side-labels), ✓/✗ matrix, task-flow diagram, SEQ bar chart, iteration before → after with arrow, spec table with raspberry header column.
- **Illustration style:** soft stock photography (mothers, babies), 3D emoji icons, angled phone mockups. No mascot, though the AI "Bibu" has a character.
- **Density:** medium-high. Justified serif paragraphs dominate many pages, with cards breaking them up.

## Why it likely won
- **Audience empathy and brand fit:** palette, photography, tone and the LENTERA metaphor all express "safe, calm space for mothers". Brand coherence reads as UX maturity.
- **Research depth on the "why":** stigma quotes, mental-load literature and the Hook Model show behavioural thinking about retention, not just features.
- **Sensitive-domain feature set** (anonymous venting, psychologist escalation, AI companion, anti-mom-shaming broadcast) answers the stigma root cause directly.
- **Rubric completeness:** all DT artefacts plus testing with 12 concrete missions and a high SUS (89.17).

## Weaknesses
- **Only one test round**, tester count missing, and SEQ scale described inconsistently ("1–5 … up to 6"). There is no quantified before/after, so the iteration claim rests on prose.
- Survey n not reported. Multiple-choice percentages without a base.
- Persona copy duplicated (Alia's goals repeated), typos ("sedangh", "Komunita"), garbled glyphs from export.
- Serif body with justified text on a UI/UX entry reduces scannability. Long paragraphs.
- Hasil yang Dicapai lists national baselines as if HiBU would reduce them, with no targets or KPIs.
- No business model or rollout. Safety/clinical risk (crisis handling, data privacy for mental health) is barely addressed.

## Reusable techniques
- Pick an **acronym that is a meaningful local word with a metaphor** (LENTERA = lantern for a dark phase). Show it as a full-width banner between the HMW row and the feature cards.
- Add a **Hook Model Canvas** whenever the product depends on habit or retention (health, finance, learning).
- Fill the empathy map with **verbatim social-pressure quotes** in the users' own slang. This is more persuasive than paraphrase.
- **Stakeholder outcome cards** (user / society / government) on the goals page. Wijaya should add KPIs to each.
- List **all SEQ missions** (10–12 tasks) and chart SEQ per mission. This shows test-plan rigour.
- **Match palette and photography to the user's emotional state** for consumer UX briefs without a sponsor.
- Use a **faded photo bleed** on section-divider pages for an editorial feel without extra content.
